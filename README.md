
# SlimX.ai

**Building AI systems that humans can inspect, steer, and trust.**

SlimX is an applied AI R&D company based in the Netherlands. We develop agentic AI systems, human–AI collaboration tools, and open-source infrastructure for building intelligent applications.

### What we build

- **Agentic AI** — Intelligent agents, tool integration, and workflow automation.
- **Human–AI Collaboration** — Systems designed for inspection, feedback, and human control.
- **RAG & Knowledge Systems** — Retrieval, document understanding, and evidence-grounded AI.
- **Applied AI R&D** — From research concepts to working prototypes and industrial applications.

### Open-source projects

- [SlimX Core](https://github.com/slimx-ai/slimx) — Lightweight library for working with LLMs.
- [SlimX-RAG](https://github.com/slimx-ai/SlimX-RAG) — Retrieval-augmented generation.
- [SlimX-Agent](https://github.com/slimx-ai/slimx-Agent) — Agentic AI development.
- [SlimX-MCP](https://github.com/slimx-ai/slimx-MCP) — Model Context Protocol tooling.

### Collaborate with us

We collaborate with research institutes, startups, and industry partners on applied AI research, open-source development, and European R&D projects.
